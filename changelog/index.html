What’s New

What changed in each version of Day, newest first. Day comes from the App Store, which delivers the updates.

2.2.4 iPhone and iPad

New features

  • Photos on events and reminders: add one on the card or in the editor. It syncs through iCloud to your other devices and shows behind the Days Counter widget.
  • Templates have their own page in Settings, where you add, edit and delete them.
  • Settings are tidier: Looking Back (Week in Review, Reminder Summary) comes first, then Manage, with much less text.
  • Apple Watch: say or type a line to add an event or reminder, check the day and time, tap the tick; your iPhone saves it.
  • Four new complications on the watch: Days Counter, the next reminder (tick it off right there), the date and the day's progress.
  • Up Next as a Live Activity in the watch's Smart Stack.
  • The watch app is simpler: each row shows only the title and time.

Fixes

  • Day view: jumping several days no longer makes the timeline shake.
  • Week in Review: calendar names no longer hide under their bars, and the whole Reminder Summary row responds to a tap.
  • iPhone Duo unfolded upright: the next-event and search bar sits evenly between the buttons.
  • iPhone Duo folded: sheets reach the edge of the screen, the invitations button sits top right, and the view menu answers taps again.

2.2.3 iPhone and iPad

New features

  • Widgets in Settings: every Day widget, drawn live with your own events in its real sizes, with how to add it to the Home Screen, Lock Screen or Control Center.
  • Week in Review: your scheduled time against the week before, hours per calendar, the busiest day, free time and reminders done, for any of the last twelve weeks.
  • Share a day or a week as a picture or as text, or only the times you're free; titles can be hidden.
  • Templates: save a kind of event or reminder once (length, calendar, alerts, place, repeat) and start a new one from it in Quick Add, the editor or Shortcuts.
  • Defaults for new events and reminders in Settings: length, alerts (as many as you like, at any time), a reminder's time and priority.
  • Touch Response in Settings: Fast, Normal or Slow, for how quickly touch and hold and dragging react.
  • Settings are sorted anew: Appearance, Up Next, Input, Default Apps, General and About; the Calendars and Reminders pages go from what's shown to new items to alerts.
  • Favorites: star an event or reminder on its card. The yellow star next to Reminders opens them all, synced through iCloud.
  • Days Counter widget: the days to an event or reminder, or since it. Pick from all or only favorites, choose a style (Classic, Bold, Calendar Page, Minimal) and a text color; also on the Lock Screen.
  • Day Counter on Cards: an option in Settings that shows the days left at the top right of an event or reminder card.
  • Look Ahead in Settings › Up Next: how far ahead an event comes first: the rest of today, the next event, 6, 12 or 24 hours, or a number of your own.
  • Up Next shows a reminder's day and time like an event's.
  • The large Up Next widget is tidier: aligned rows, a list that fills the space and a clear empty state.
  • Reminders with a date but no time can have an alert or be urgent; they go off at 9:00.
  • A new app icon, and new date icons for every day of the month.
  • The Habits widget is gone.

Fixes

  • Ticking off a reminder that has only a date, before its day, now asks first.
  • Saving or deleting no longer reloads the whole calendar.
  • Spotlight and notifications are prepared in the background, so Day stays responsive.
  • Up Next redraws only when the next item changes, which saves battery.
  • Months, weeks and the year redraw less when you swipe.

  • Templates: save a kind of event or reminder once (length, calendar, alerts, place, repeat) and start a new one from it in Quick Add, the editor or Shortcuts. On the Mac also from a day's right-click menu.
  • Defaults for new events and reminders in Settings: length, alerts (as many as you like, at any time), a reminder's time and priority.
  • Settings are sorted anew: Appearance, Up Next, Input, Default Apps, General and About; the Calendars and Reminders pages go from what's shown to new items to alerts.
  • Resizing the window is smooth: the calendar follows while you drag and no longer falls behind.
  • Switching months is much faster, with a new month calendar in the sidebar.
  • A tidier top bar: ‹ Today › and New Event with search sit in two capsules, and the bar no longer overflows. The sidebar follows the window's width.
  • Dictate in any language: Day now understands speech on this device with an optional Qwen model, detects the language by itself and no longer drops words after a pause.
  • Reminder lists in the calendar: show a whole list as one element a day, with its progress.
  • Reminder lists are sorted by day: Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, then each date; reminders without a date come last.
  • Rename reminder lists, and add a list right from the editor.
  • New look for the Reminder Summary and the reminder card.
  • Favorites: star an event or reminder on its card. The yellow star next to Reminders opens them all, synced through iCloud.
  • Days Counter widget: the days to an event or reminder, or since it, in four styles (Classic, Bold, Calendar Page, Minimal) and a text color of your choice.
  • Day Counter on Cards: an option in Settings that shows the days left at the top right of an event or reminder card.
  • Look Ahead in Settings › Up Next: how far ahead an event comes first: the rest of today, the next event, 6, 12 or 24 hours, or a number of your own.
  • Reminders with a date but no time can have an alert or be urgent; they go off at 9:00.
  • On-Device AI in Settings: choose the engines for Quick Add and dictation and manage the models.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts in Settings: see every shortcut and change it.
  • A new welcome tour and a new app icon.
  • What's New now opens inside the app.
  • The Habits widget is gone.

Fixes

  • Ticking off a reminder that has only a date, before its day, now asks first.
  • A renamed or recolored calendar shows its new name and color in the sidebar at once.
  • Saving or deleting no longer reloads the whole calendar.
  • Spotlight and notifications are prepared in the background, so Day stays responsive.
  • The now line in the month no longer sits under the habits.
  • List order and the habit graph sync through iCloud.

2.2.1 iPhone and iPad

New features

  • iPhone Duo: Day uses the whole unfolded screen. Up Next and New Event sit at the bottom, and the month shows the day's events in a column on the right.
  • iPhone Duo with the system strip beside it: Day's buttons move into the strip (search, views, New Event, Today, Reminders and the month's (i)). The strip follows light and dark mode, and the next event spans the area beside it.
  • One fixed bottom bar on iPhone and in small iPad windows: views, the next event and search, with New Event at the bottom right next to search. The options to move the bar's buttons are gone.
  • Search opens from its button into a field above the keyboard, with a round button to close it. On iPad, search sits in the top bar.
  • iPad: the view switcher sits right after the title, Reminders has its own button in the top bar, and the header no longer has a Settings button.
  • Reminder Summary: an activity graph of the last weeks, the share done on time as a ring, a chart by list and a short note about your week.
  • What's New follows the app's language, and a language picked there is kept.
  • Up Next widget: a reminder with a time that comes first shows as the big card, like an event.

Fixes

  • Folding, unfolding and turning the Duo: sheets and search close cleanly, the window gets its full frame back, and the bottom bar no longer goes missing after many changes.
  • iPad: in a small window turned sideways, Day no longer resizes itself to full screen, and the title keeps clear of the window controls.
  • Smoother: swiping between days and months redraws less, and event menus are built only when you open them.

2.1.6 iPhone and iPad

New features

  • Habits: any repeating reminder can be a habit. Set a weekly goal (for example 4 times a week) and Day tracks the current streak, the longest streak, how many days you did it and missed, and in how many weeks you met the goal.
  • Forgiving streak: a day you skip on purpose is excused. It counts as neither done nor missed, so it does not break the streak.
  • At the End of the Day: choose per reminder what Day does when the day is over — leave it open, mark it completed, or (for repeating reminders) skip it. Day applies this as of the day the reminder was due, when you open the app and in the refresh iOS allows after midnight. A day that ended while neither ran is settled the next time. Ticking a settled reminder back on is never undone again. The rules stay on this device.
  • Values: log an amount per day on a habit's card (8 km, 20 min) with a unit, and see a line of the last 30 days.
  • Weekly habit graph (Settings > Reminders > Habit Graph, off by default): replaces the 70-day square grid on a repeating reminder's card with one bar per week showing how much of it was done. Streak, done and missed figures stay.
  • Today's habits sit at the top of the day's list.
  • New Habits widget (small and medium): lists today's open habits, each with a button that ticks it off, as in the app.
  • Find Free Time (Calendar menu, ⇧⌘F with a keyboard): a sheet lists the free stretches of the next five days, 8:00 to 20:00 and from now on today, for a length of 15 minutes to 2 hours. Tap a gap to start a new event at its beginning. Only this device's calendars count.
  • Settings > What's New now opens the notes from Day's public site in the app, so a new version's notes show up without an app update. Offline, the notes bundled with the app show instead. Nothing about you is sent; the site only sees a normal web request.
  • Habits and Find Free Time are translated into all ten languages.

Fixes

  • Up Next, Reminders and Invitations: a card picked from the list now opens in front. On the Mac it opened hidden behind the sheet.

2.1.6 Mac

New features

  • Everything in iPhone and iPad 2.1.6: Habits, the Habits widget, Find Free Time (⇧⌘F) and What's New from the public site.
  • Since Mac 2.1.3, also: pull down on a timeline or press ⌘R to refresh calendars and reminders (Refresh is in the View menu), and the Mac now keeps every view you have opened and warms the others after launch, so switching between Day, 3 Days, Week, Month and Year is a show, not a rebuild (median switch hitch measured 110 ms before, 39 ms now).

Fixes

  • Search: the field takes everything you type. It used to collapse to a magnifier after the first letter. Escape still leaves search, and ⌘F focuses it.
  • Month: the '+n more' list is a floating panel like the event card, so a click outside closes it and still reaches what is under it. Escape or a second click on the button also closes it, and a click on a row opens that event's card.
  • The event card, the Month's '+n more' list and cards picked from Up Next, Reminders or Invitations open in front of the window and of sheets, not behind them.
  • A swipe on a day's list no longer taps New Event. The top bar is one line that shrinks, with a single Today stepper, and clicks land where you aim.
