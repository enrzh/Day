Your day, at a glance.

Day shows the calendars you already have, iCloud, Google, Exchange and more, together with your Reminders. One calm app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

A calendar that stays out of the way

Day doesn't ask you to move anywhere. It reads and writes the calendars on your device, so what you change in Day is what every other app shows.

  • Every view you need

    Day, 3 Days, Week, Month and Year. Pinch to move between them, and zoom the hours on the timeline. Calendar colors and time zones make events easy to place.

  • Add an event in one line

    Type or say “Lunch with Anna tomorrow at 1pm” and check what Day understood before it's added. Dates and times are read on your device, with Apple Intelligence or an optional on-device model for more details.

  • Reminders, right in the calendar

    Reminders from Apple's Reminders app that have a date appear next to your events. Tick them off, drag them to a new time, and keep your lists as cards, with groups of your own.

  • What's next, everywhere

    Widgets from small to large, on the Home Screen and Lock Screen. A Live Activity with a countdown and Join or Directions. Controls for Control Center and the Action button.

  • Made for iPad and Mac

    Bigger screens get bigger views: event names in every day of the month, seven full week columns. On the Mac there is a menu bar, keyboard shortcuts, and popovers like Calendar's.

  • On your wrist

    The Apple Watch app shows your agenda with event details, and complications put what's on now or next on the watch face.

  • Places and travel time

    Pick a place from Apple Maps and Day can tell you when to leave. Join links for Zoom, Meet, Teams and more open in the right app.

  • Private by design

    No account, no server, no tracking. Your events stay in your calendars. Day's privacy manifest declares no data collection.

  • In your language

    English, German, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian and Korean.

One app, three screens

The same Day on iPhone, iPad and Mac, each laid out for how you hold it.

iPad

All orientations, Split View and Stage Manager. Month shows event names in each day, Week has room for seven full columns, and the keyboard shortcuts work as you'd expect.

Mac

A real Mac app, with ⌘N for a new event, ⌘T for today, ⌘1 to ⌘5 for the views, ⌘F for search and ⌘, for settings. Click an event for a popover pointing at it, and drag to move.

Help

Day shows no events, or not all of them.

Day needs full access to your calendars: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → Day → “Full Access”. In Day, choose the calendars to show under Settings → Calendars.

How do I add a Google or Exchange calendar?

Add the account in iOS Settings under Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts. Day shows it automatically. Events sync through those accounts, not through Day.

Where are my events stored?

In your own calendars. Day has no server of its own and doesn't copy events. What you change in Day goes straight into that calendar.

How do I see my Reminders in Day?

Turn on Reminders in Day's settings (Day asks once when you first open it) and choose which lists to show. Reminders with a date then appear in the calendar. Ticking one off in Day completes it in Reminders.

Why aren't my Reminders groups there?

Apple doesn't share Reminders' groups with other apps. Day has its own groups for your lists. They live in Day and don't change anything in Reminders.

What is the on-device model for Quick Add?

Quick Add reads dates and times with the system's detector, and with Apple Intelligence it also picks out the title, place and repeat. If you want that without Apple Intelligence, you can download a small language model once under Settings → Quick Add. It runs on your device, so what you type doesn't leave it.

How do travel times and leave-by alerts work?

For events with a place, Day can ask Apple Maps when you need to leave and remind you in time. For this Day needs your location while the app is in use. Turn it on under Settings → Reminders → Leave-by Alerts.

Can I use Google Maps or another browser?

Yes. Under Settings → Default Apps you choose what opens directions and links.

How do my settings stay the same on all devices?

With Settings → General → “Sync Settings with iCloud”, selected settings follow your iCloud to your other devices. Which calendars are shown stays per device.
